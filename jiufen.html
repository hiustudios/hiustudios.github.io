Jiufen Old Street

2026

Kennard Alvaro Hadinata

Born 2008
From Surabaya, Indonesia
Medium Ink on paper
Dimensions 60 x 80 cm
Status Not For Sale

About the Artwork

This is a painting of a town called Jiufen. It's located in Taiwan and is especially beautiful at night because of its many bright lanterns. These features make it look like it's from studio ghibli's movie, "Spirited Away", hence the many references in the painting.

About the Artist

Kennard is an artist who loves cats.
